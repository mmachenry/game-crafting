I grabbed these face card images from 
../originals/card_faces_standard_american_52/

and I used inkscape to remove the bottom suit pips from each card
and also create an image of just the bottom suit pips seperately.

I then opened the images with the card face that were missing their 
bottom pips and imported the opposite bottom pip. For example I opened
the original image of the Queen of Hearts missing the bottom pips and then
imported the image of the upside down bottom pips for diamonds.

Then they were never centered on 0,0 so I moved them to 0,0. Two cards
looked weird when I did this. JS with C on the bottom and QS with C on the
bottom. I adjusted them 2.5 to the right and it was okay.

I must note that the bottom pips might not be located exactly in the same spot on all cards. For example the large club on the king of clubs might not
be exactly in the same x,y location as the giant club on the jack of clubs
and it might also be a different size. I don't remember which card I grabbed
the sample bottom suit pip from for each of the three options (J,Q,K) for 
each.
